# Logo Design Prompt for iWantSexNow.ch

## Project Overview
**Website Name:** iWantSexNow.ch  
**Industry:** Premium adult dating and escort services platform  
**Target Market:** Switzerland (Zürich, Geneva, Basel, Bern, Lausanne)  
**Target Audience:** Affluent professionals aged 25-55 seeking discreet, premium companionship

## Brand Positioning
- **Primary Tagline:** "Where Desire Meets Discretion"
- **Brand Personality:** Sophisticated, discreet, premium, trustworthy, elegant
- **Tone:** Refined luxury with modern sensibility - NOT explicit or vulgar
- **Competitive Positioning:** High-end, professional, exclusive (think luxury hotel or private club)

## Current Design Aesthetic
The website uses:
- **Color Palette:** Subdued and sophisticated
  - Primary: Charcoal blacks, deep grays (#1a1a1a, #2C2C2C)
  - Accents: Muted burgundy/rose (#6B1B3D, subtle rose tones)
  - Neutrals: Warm grays, cream tones
- **Design Style:** Refined minimalism, elegant, not colorful or flashy
- **Visual Language:** Clean lines, sophisticated typography, subtle luxury cues

## Logo Requirements

### Style Direction
Create a logo that conveys:
1. **Discretion & Privacy** - Subtle, not overtly sexual
2. **Luxury & Exclusivity** - Premium feel, high-end aesthetic
3. **Trust & Professionalism** - Legitimate, safe, verified
4. **Modern Sophistication** - Contemporary, not dated
5. **Swiss Quality** - Precision, refinement, excellence

### Design Preferences
- **Icon/Mark:** Abstract, geometric, or symbolic (NOT literal hearts, explicit imagery)
- **Style:** Minimalist, elegant, timeless
- **Complexity:** Simple enough to work at small sizes (16x16px favicon to large formats)
- **Versatility:** Must work in monochrome and full color

### Potential Design Directions (Choose One or Combine Elements)
1. **Geometric Abstract:** Interlocking shapes suggesting connection/intimacy
2. **Monogram:** Stylized "I" and "W" or "S" and "N" letterforms
3. **Symbolic:** Abstract representation of desire/connection (think: intertwined elements, subtle curves)
4. **Architectural:** Swiss-inspired precision with warm, inviting elements
5. **Minimalist Icon:** Simple, memorable mark (like a flame, keyhole, or abstract embrace)

### Technical Specifications
- **Format:** Vector-based design (SVG-ready)
- **Dimensions:** Square or slightly horizontal (1:1 or 4:3 ratio)
- **Color Versions Needed:**
  - Full color (2-3 colors max)
  - Monochrome (black)
  - Monochrome (white for dark backgrounds)
- **Size Requirements:** Scalable from 16px to 500px

### Color Guidance
**Option 1 - Monochromatic Elegance:**
- Primary: Charcoal black (#2C2C2C)
- Accent: Warm gray (#6B7280)

**Option 2 - Sophisticated Contrast:**
- Primary: Deep burgundy (#6B1B3D)
- Secondary: Champagne gold (#D4AF37) - use sparingly
- Base: Charcoal (#2C2C2C)

**Option 3 - Modern Minimal:**
- Primary: Pure black (#000000)
- Accent: Muted rose (#8B5F6F)

### Typography Considerations (if text is included)
- **Font Style:** Modern sans-serif or elegant serif
- **Weight:** Bold/Black for impact, or Light/Thin for sophistication
- **Suggested Fonts:** Playfair Display, Cormorant, Montserrat, Inter, or similar
- **Text Treatment:** Consider using only "IWSN" as monogram or stylized wordmark

## What to AVOID
❌ Explicit sexual imagery  
❌ Cheap/tacky aesthetic (no neon, no cartoon style)  
❌ Overly feminine or masculine bias  
❌ Cliché symbols (lips, high heels, obvious hearts)  
❌ Busy/complex designs that don't scale down  
❌ Bright, saturated colors (pink, purple, bright red)  
❌ Playful or cute elements

- **Premium Fashion:** Chanel, Hermès (elegant, timeless)

The logo needs to convey trust and exclusivity while maintaining appropriate subtlety for the industry.

- ✓ Work on both light and dark backgrounds
